## Natural Earth reference points

map-places.json contains reference coordinates and city name aliases from ne_110m_admin_0_countries and ne_10m_populated_places.
Original country-reference source revision: 9380cca83db5f9aef52d5e762765100745f84b27
Original city source revision: 789c9904087846cc3361302857aa2e76b0ae71ff
All Natural Earth data is public domain: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
City and country/region reference coordinates are approximate; they do not locate a person or IP host.
